How to play

Controls

  • Swipe / ← → / A D — change lane
  • Swipe up / Space / W — jump
  • Swipe down / S / ↓ — slide
  • P / Esc — pause

Power-ups

  • Turbo — fly faster and smash straight through obstacles
  • Coin Magnet — pulls nearby coins to you
  • Shield — survives one hit (or a beast attack)
  • 2x Coins — every coin counts double

Survive the trail

  • Jump over fire pits, stone walls, spikes and fallen logs.
  • Slide under hanging vines, banners — and under a fireball.
  • Dodge statues, tiki idols and flame barriers sideways.
  • Demon monkeys throw fireballs: slide or switch lane fast.
  • Clip an obstacle at the edge and you stumble — the monkeys close in.
  • The golden idol is worth 500 points and 25 coins.

World

  • Three zones: Deep Jungle, Ancient Ruins, Temple of the Idol.
  • The longer you run, the faster the temple gets.
